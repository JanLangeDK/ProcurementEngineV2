# Preferred suppliers

## Choosing between suppliers

When two suppliers hold a valid contract price for the material, the lower price wins. When the
prices differ by less than 3%, the preferred supplier below wins. The reason is always stated to
the requester.

## Preferred per category

| Category | Preferred | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Packaging | Nordpak Emballage A/S (S1001) | Film is stocked in both widths and delivery is reliable |
| Packaging, film and wrap | Danfilm Plast A/S (S1002) | Fast on stretch wrap and shrink bags |
| Spices and casings | Krydderihuset ApS (S1004) | Certificates of analysis with every lot |
| Cleaning chemicals | Jysk Rengøringskemi ApS (S1009) | Widest range; two late deliveries in 2026 are on file |
| Logistics supplies | Frisk Logistik A/S (S1015) | Pallet exchange at the dock |
| Maintenance parts | Teknisk Reservedel A/S (S1020) | Same-day courier before 10:00 |

## Never

Never place an order with a supplier whose contract has expired or whose block flag is set,
even if the price is lower.
