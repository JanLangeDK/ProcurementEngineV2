# Invoice handling

## Tolerances

Price: a line may differ from the contract price by up to 1% of the contract line value or 200 DKK,
whichever is higher. Within that, the invoice is acceptable and is parked with a note citing the
tolerance. Above it, the invoice is blocked and the supplier is asked.

Quantity: tolerance 0. An invoice may not bill more than was received on the goods receipt.

## Matching

Always use the matcher's numbers. Never add up an invoice yourself. A missing goods receipt is
not a match: the goods may not have arrived.

## Resolving a deviation

A price deviation is resolved by a credit note from the supplier or by the supplier confirming
the contract price. A credit note is recorded as an invoice note, with its number. The invoice is
not released before that. A quantity deviation is resolved when the requester confirms the goods
arrived or the goods receipt is posted.

## Terms and payment

Default payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date. Payment runs weekly. Payment run weekday: thursday.
The expected payment date is the first payment run on or after the due date.

## Escalation

After two rounds without a reply, hand the case back with both attempts in the reason. An invoice
from a blocked supplier is blocked and the manager is told.
