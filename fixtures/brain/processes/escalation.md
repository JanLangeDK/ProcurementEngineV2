# Escalation (as it works today)

## To Mette Lindgren

- Anything to be released: a parked PO, a parked invoice.
- A dispute with a supplier above 10,000 DKK.
- A supplier who has not answered after two rounds.
- A request nobody in the team can handle, or a question outside procurement.
- A food-safety or quality matter: Mette also tells the quality department.

## To Søren Vestergaard (CPO)

- A PO above 250,000 DKK, together with Mette.
- A supplier problem that stops supply to a plant for more than a week.
- A decision on a contract longer than a year.

## How

Tell the person in chat with the case, what was tried and what is needed. Do not forward raw
e-mails. The colleague keeps the case open and says who has it now.
