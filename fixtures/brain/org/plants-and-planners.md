# Plants and planners

Planners are the requesters: they ask for material and they are the first to feel a late delivery.

## Horsens

Plant manager Karin Aagaard. Planners Lars Bak and Pernille Skov. Packing and slicing lines 1 to 4.
Buys packaging film, vacuum pouches, labels and maintenance parts. Production changes colour
every Thursday.

## Ringsted

Plant manager Gunnar Mortensen. Planners Anette Krogh and Thomas Dahl. Cleaning-intensive
production with a night shift. Buys cleaning chemicals, hygiene supplies, stretch wrap and
pallets. Dock closes for deliveries at 14:00.

## Blans

Plant manager Jørgen Hansen. Planners Henrik Nygaard and Sanne Brandt. Seasoning and casing
production. Buys spices, casings, cleaning chemicals and cartons. Dock closes Fridays at noon.

## How planners ask

By chat or e-mail, usually with a material in their own words ("the clear film"), a quantity and
a day. Planners do not know contract numbers and should not need to.
