# The procurement team

The department is small. Four AI colleagues work alongside these people, and every AI colleague
has one human manager: **Mette Lindgren**.

## Colleague manager

Mette Lindgren, Head of Procurement Operations. She releases parked purchase orders and parked
invoices, decides what the AI colleagues may do, and is the first escalation for everything.
Her backup when she is away is Jesper Holm (see `approval-chain.md`).

## Category managers

Each category has one human who owns the supplier relationship, the contract and the decision
when something falls outside the contracts.

| Category | Category manager |
| --- | --- |
| Packaging | Jesper Holm |
| Spices and casings | Karen Mølgaard |
| Cleaning chemicals | Ole Winther |
| Logistics supplies | Rikke Sand |
| Maintenance parts | Bo Frederiksen |

## Above the team

Søren Vestergaard is the Chief Procurement Officer. He is told about anything that threatens
supply to a plant for more than a week and about any contract decision above one year.

## The AI colleagues

Concierge (routes every message), Jonas the Buyer, Sofie the AP specialist, Noah the Supplier
manager. All four report to Mette Lindgren.
